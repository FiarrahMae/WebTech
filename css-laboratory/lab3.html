Why Readable Web Pages Matter

Typography

Good typography makes text easy to scan and comfortable to read. A clean sans-serif font, a sensible size, and generous spacing all help visitors stay focused on the content.

Line height is especially important. When lines are packed too tightly, the eye struggles to move from one line to the next.

Color and Contrast

Text should always stand out clearly from its background. Dark text on a light surface is a reliable choice, and a small, consistent palette keeps the design calm and professional.

  • Choose a readable, sans-serif font
  • Use a comfortable line height
  • Keep text and background colors in strong contrast
  • Limit the palette to a few consistent colors
